# SansApp Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-15_

**SansApp collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no
tracking, and no advertising.

- **Bluetooth / MIDI** is used only to connect to your Tech 21 pedal and exchange MIDI messages
  with it. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and nothing leaves your device.
- **Your presets and cabs** stay on your device. Backup files (`.p3b`) are written locally and are
  shared only when you explicitly choose to share or export them.
- **No data is collected, stored on a server, or transmitted to the developer or any third party.**

SansApp is free and open source (GPL-3.0). Questions or concerns:
https://github.com/mckelveygreg/sansapp/issues
