SansApp is a MIDI editor for the Tech 21 SansAmp Programmable Bass Driver DI Elite — a hardware bass
preamp pedal. The editing features require the physical pedal, connected via a Bluetooth or USB MIDI
adapter, which the review team will not have.

Without a pedal connected, the app launches to a "Connect" screen and every screen remains browsable
(the editor, IR page, presets, backup and settings all render). The app needs no account or login
and collects no data.

Please note that on those screens the knobs and switches are intentionally shown dimmed and do not
respond to touch while no pedal is connected, and Device Settings shows "—" in place of values. This
is deliberate, not a fault: those controls send their value to the hardware as you move them, so with
nothing connected a moving control would be reporting a change that never happened. Each page states
the connection status at the top. Tapping a knob still opens its detail page, and the graphs, preset
list, backup and settings screens are all fully viewable, so the app can be navigated end to end
without the hardware.

This is an independent, unofficial project. The Tech 21 / SansAmp / Bass Driver names are used
nominatively to describe hardware compatibility. Source and full description:
https://github.com/mckelveygreg/sansapp
