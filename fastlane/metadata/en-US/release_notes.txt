Works with pedal firmware 1.0, 1.1 and 1.2.

New:
• Read from Pedal. Turned some knobs at the pedal without saving? Read from Pedal brings those tweaks into the app, so what you see matches what you hear. Afterwards it tells you plainly whether the read checked out. One limit, from the pedal itself: an engaged Red Zone can't be carried across, so turn it back on at the footswitch.
• The IR page now says what each cab slot holds. Rows are named for the cab stored there, such as "Factory cab 3", "Your cab", or "No cab stored", and slots 7 and 8 only get a tag when something is wrong: EMPTY or READ FAILED. A caption under the graph says where the curve you're looking at came from, and marks it "approximate" when it's a stand-in for the factory cab.
• Settings → Behaviour → Confirm before switching presets. Turn it off and changing presets discards unsaved edits without asking, which is handy while you experiment. It's on by default.

Fixed:
• Renaming the preset you're playing now sticks. Before, saving afterwards could put the old name back.
• Tapping an amp model no longer changes your preset's output level. Before, VT Bass could drop the level to silence and Bass Driver could jump it to full.
• An amp model's button now shows "edited" when your preset is voiced from that model but its knobs have moved off it, and explains that tapping it resets the voicing to the factory recipe.
• A user IR slot can't be switched on unless a cab is really stored where it points. An upload that stopped partway could leave a pointer at empty memory, which the pedal plays back very loud.
• The IR graph no longer shows a neighbouring cab's curve when the mic sits between a cab it has read and one it hasn't. It shows nothing and says which cab is missing.
